## Glossary Workflow

### Before starting a chapter:
1. Read the full glossary
2. Note which terms from the glossary appear in this chapter
3. These MUST use the established translation — no variation

### During translation:
1. When you encounter a new term that will recur, **pause** and add it to glossary
2. If you're unsure, mark as [TENTATIVE] and add to Open Questions
3. Don't use different translations for the same term in the same chapter

### After completing a chapter:
1. Review any [TENTATIVE] entries — are they confirmed now?
2. Add any new Recurring Phrases discovered
3. Move resolved Open Questions to appropriate section

### Resolving TENTATIVE terms:
If a term appears in 5+ places and you've established a pattern, change [TENTATIVE] to [CONFIRMED]. If you realize a TENTATIVE choice was wrong, add it to Rejected Translations.

## What Deserves a Glossary Entry?

**YES — always add:**
- Proper nouns (people, places, organizations, brands)
- Technical domain terms (field-specific vocabulary)
- Terms the author coined or uses in special ways
- Recurring phrases (3+ times across chapters)
- Any term where you had to make a non-obvious choice

**NO — don't add:**
- Common everyday words
- Terms with one obvious Vietnamese equivalent
- Terms that only appear once
