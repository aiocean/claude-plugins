What holds the train

We judge a serializer regression very likely, 20-45% by our own count, and rate a network cause unlikely: 80-95%.

Source: https://github.com/acme/checkout/blob/main/src/serialize.ts#L41-L46, mirrored at pool.ts, trunk.

The rise reproduces on every host in the pool, so a single degraded machine does not account for it. The release train holds until the flame graph on build 830 names the frame that grew. The serializer landed in build 826 and is the only change on the hot path between the two builds under comparison, which is the reason the pool-wide shape of the regression matters more than any single host reading. Thirty minutes of steady-state load per host is enough to separate a warm-cache artefact from a real change in the work the request does, and the four hosts agree to within eleven milliseconds at p99.
