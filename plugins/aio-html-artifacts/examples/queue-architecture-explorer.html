Recommendation

Choose B for this team and horizon.

The retry ledger clears the recovery SLO while staying inside the delivery constraint. Accept one new reconciliation loop; shadow it before authority moves.

Reconsider if: the measured maintenance load exceeds 0.2 FTE or the workflow engine becomes an already-operated shared platform.

Assumptions, unknowns, and evidence limits
  • Observed: options A and B were load-tested with identical payloads.
  • Unknown: option C recovery is a vendor benchmark, not locally reproduced.
  • Assumption: the team remains six engineers for the next two quarters.
  • Decision owner: Platform lead. Review horizon: six months.
