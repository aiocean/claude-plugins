Change the dispute cohort without stranding a shop mid-journey

The webhook cohort stays at or below the scope cohort

In the checked-in file, dispute_scope ships to 20% of shops and dispute_webhook to 10%.

Of the 1,842 shops inside the scope cohort over the last 14 days, 1,124 granted the scope, or 61.0%.

Raising dispute_webhook above dispute_scope strands every shop in the gap: the webhook fires for a shop that was never asked for the scope, delivery returns 403, and the reconciliation job retries until someone drains the queue by hand likely (55-80%).

Set the webhook cohort at or below the scope cohort in the controls below, export the diff, and paste it into the flag change request.

I assumed both flags hash the shop id with the same salt, so a shop inside the 10% webhook cohort is inside the 20% scope cohort. I did not verify the salt against the flag service.
